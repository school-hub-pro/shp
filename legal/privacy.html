Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

1. About this project

School Hub Pro is an independently developed study tool. It is not a commercial product and is not affiliated with any school, company, or organisation. Anyone can create an account and use the app for free.

2. Why this app is safe to use

We know it can feel risky to type a password into a site you have not seen before, so here are the guarantees that matter most:

  • We cannot read your password. It is hashed the moment you submit it, using the same one-way algorithm that password managers use. Only the hash is stored, and the hash cannot be reversed back into your password.
  • We never ask for payment details. No subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no card numbers. The app is completely free.
  • We never ask for personal identifiers. No NRIC, no home address, no phone number, no government ID. Just a display name and an email address.
  • We never ask for device permissions. No access to your camera, microphone, files, contacts, or location. If a browser prompt ever appears asking for these on our site, close the tab — it is not coming from us.
  • You can leave at any time. One button in Settings permanently deletes your account and everything tied to it.
  • You can verify this yourself. The frontend code is public on the GitHub repository linked in the app's footer.

3. What we collect

When you create an account and use School Hub Pro, the following is stored:

  • Account details — your display name, email address, and a securely hashed copy of your password. The password is never stored in plain text and cannot be read by anyone, including the developer.
  • Content you add — grades, deadlines, personal calendar events, focus sessions, academic goals, and bug reports.
  • Basic usage logs — sign-in times and general actions such as "grades saved", kept for security and troubleshooting. Passwords and message contents are never logged.

4. What we do not collect

No location data, no device identifiers, no browsing history, no analytics, no advertising IDs, and no cookies for tracking. Nothing is collected outside what you enter into the app.

5. Where your data is stored

Data is stored in a private spreadsheet owned by the developer, accessed through a Google-hosted backend. Access is restricted to the developer. Google's own privacy policy applies to the underlying infrastructure.

6. Who can see your data

  • You — full access to your own data.
  • The developer — can view accounts and content for support, safety, and maintenance.
  • Other users — cannot see your data. The only exception is your display name, and only if you accept a friend request.

Your data is never sold, shared, or transferred to any third party.

7. How long it is kept

Data is kept while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Danger Zone, which permanently removes everything associated with it.

8. Your rights

You can export everything you have added at any time from Settings → Data Export, in CSV, JSON, or calendar format. You can request a correction or a full deletion by asking the developer directly.

9. Security

Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 using per-account salts. Sessions use signed tokens with expiry dates. All traffic is encrypted over HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password that you do not reuse elsewhere.

10. Children

School Hub Pro is designed for students and is intended to be used with the knowledge of a parent or guardian. Because it stores only a display name and an email address, it collects no more personal information than is necessary for the app to function.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, users will be told through the app. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact

For any privacy question or data request, contact the developer directly using the contact link in the app's footer or the email address shown on the GitHub repository.
