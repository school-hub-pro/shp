Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

1. About this project

School Hub Pro is an independently developed study tool, shared by invitation with a small group of users. It is not a commercial product and is not affiliated with any school, company, or organisation. Access is limited to invited users.

2. What we collect

When you use School Hub Pro, the following is stored:

  • Account details — your display name, email address, and a securely hashed copy of your password. The password is never stored in plain text and cannot be read by anyone, including the owner.
  • Content you add — grades, deadlines, personal calendar events, focus sessions, academic goals, and bug reports.
  • Basic usage logs — sign-in times and general actions (e.g. "grades saved"), kept for security and troubleshooting. Passwords and message contents are never logged.

3. What we do NOT collect

No location data, no device identifiers, no browsing history, no analytics, no advertising IDs, and no cookies for tracking. Nothing is collected outside what you enter into the app.

4. Where your data is stored

Data is stored in a private Google Sheets spreadsheet owned by the project owner, accessed through a Google Apps Script backend. Access is restricted to the owner. Google's own privacy policy applies to the underlying infrastructure.

5. Who can see your data

  • You — full access to your own data.
  • The project owner — can view accounts and content for support, safety, and maintenance.
  • Other members — cannot see your data. The only exception is your display name, and only if you accept a friend request.

Your data is never sold, shared, or transferred to any third party.

6. How long it is kept

Data is kept while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Danger Zone, which permanently removes everything associated with it.

7. Your rights

You can export everything you have added at any time from Settings → Data Export (CSV, JSON, or calendar format). You can request a correction or full deletion by asking the project owner directly.

8. Security

Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 using per-account salts. Sessions use signed tokens with expiry dates. All traffic is encrypted over HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password that you do not reuse elsewhere.

9. Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, members will be told through the app. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

For any privacy question or data request, contact the project owner directly.
